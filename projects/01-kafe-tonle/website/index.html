Some days call for a quiet corner; others for a table full of friends. We dreamed up Kafé Tonlé as an easygoing place for both.

Inspired by the river

We imagine each branch as a place where the pace softens and conversations have time to grow.

Made for your taste

Less sugar? More ice? Extra espresso? Small choices make the drink feel like yours.

Designed for everyday convenience

Our QR ordering concept keeps the experience personal, while cutting down the wait between ordering and enjoying.

Rewards

Your 10th drink is on us.

Collect one stamp for each eligible paid drink. After nine stamps, redeem a free eligible drink on your tenth. Your reward stays in your account until used.

FAQs

Good questions.

Do I need an app to order?

No. Open the table QR link in your browser and you can order as a guest.

Can I change sugar and ice?

Yes. Choose your preference on eligible drinks before adding them to your cart.

Can I pay with KHQR?

This concept shows a simulated KHQR flow; it does not accept real payments.

Can I collect stamps without an account?

You can order as a guest, but a linked demo rewards account is required to retain stamps.

What happens if a drink is sold out?

The menu reflects availability at your selected demo branch.
