# WEBSITE.md — BrightBridge English Centre

> A vibrant but credible fictional two-branch English center website and parent/student portal, with complete public copy, course data, online enrollment and placement-test booking. `SYSTEM.md` governs enrollment, timetable, attendance, fees and grades. All student/guardian/teacher records are fake.

**Name:** BrightBridge English Centre · **Tagline:** `Your bridge to the world.` Brand colors royal blue `#2250A4`, sunny yellow `#F7C543`, crisp white `#FFFFFF`, navy `#172E54`, pale sky `#F0F6FF`. Friendly rounded headlines, clean cards and illustrated accents; age-appropriate **not childish** for teens/IELTS.
